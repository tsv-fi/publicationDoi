# Publication DOIs

Adds a DOIs tab to the publication view of the workflow. The tab lists the publication and its related items that can have a DOI according to the DOI settings (galleys in OJS and OPS; chapters, publication formats and their files in OMP) and shows their DOIs. Anyone who can see the publication tab can see the list. DOIs are assigned in DOI management as before.

The tab is shown when DOIs are enabled.

Supports OJS, OMP and OPS 3.4.
